Important Before Signing Any Contract

Before signing an important contract or agreement, carefully verify the identity and authority of the parties, ownership or rights involved, payment terms, duration, responsibilities, conditions, termination provisions and dispute-resolution arrangements.

A legal review before signing can help identify unclear terms, potential risks and obligations. The appropriate form and legal requirements depend on the nature of the transaction and applicable law.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about contracts and agreements in Nepal.

What is a contract?

A contract is a legally relevant agreement between parties creating rights and obligations according to its terms and applicable law.

Why should I have an agreement reviewed by a lawyer?

Legal review can help identify unclear terms, important obligations, potential risks and provisions that may require clarification before signing.

Can you prepare a house rent agreement?

Yes. Legal assistance can be provided for house, room, office and shop rental agreements, subject to the applicable legal requirements.

Can you prepare a land lease agreement?

Yes. Land lease agreements can be drafted or reviewed based on the proposed transaction, rights, obligations and applicable law.

Can you review a property sale and purchase agreement?

Yes. A property-related agreement can be reviewed for important terms, responsibilities, conditions and potential legal issues.

What happens if one party breaches a contract?

The appropriate legal response depends on the contract, facts, evidence and applicable law. Legal consultation can help determine the available options.

Can a contract be changed after signing?

A contract may sometimes be amended or renewed by agreement of the parties, subject to the original contract and applicable law.

Should I sign an agreement without reading it carefully?

No. Important agreements should be read and understood carefully. Where appropriate, obtain legal advice before signing.
